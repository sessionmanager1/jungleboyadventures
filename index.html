Field Log — Manyatta, Southern Mara

The Manyatta Visit

Step inside a living Maasai homestead. A Manyatta visit is an invitation into daily life — mud-and-dung homes built by hand, cattle kraals at the centre of the compound, and generations of knowledge passed down around the fire.

Meet the families who call the Mara home, hear the meaning behind the beadwork worn by every age-set, and understand how this community has coexisted with the wildlife around it for centuries.

Add to My Safari
